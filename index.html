About the studio

Technology should make life feel lighter, not louder.

Baker Studios is an independent software company creating apps for Apple platforms. We focus on useful ideas, intuitive interfaces, and careful craftsmanship from the first sketch to every update that follows.

We believe the best digital tools earn their place: they’re understandable, dependable, and respectful of the people who use them.
