Privacy Policy

Effective August 10, 2026

Last updated August 10, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Baker Studios LLC, doing business as Baker Studios (“Baker Studios,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you visit this website or use a Baker Studios app that links to this policy.

The short version: This website does not use advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, accounts, or cookies. We only receive personal information if you choose to contact us.

Information this website handles

Information you send us

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, name, message, and any attachments. We use it only to respond, provide support, maintain appropriate business records, and protect our rights.

Basic server logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider and web server may automatically create short-lived technical logs when a page is requested. These logs may include an IP address, browser or device type, requested URL, referring page, and date and time. We use this information to keep the site reliable and secure, diagnose errors, and prevent abuse. We do not use it to build advertising profiles or track you across services.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies and does not use third-party analytics or advertising services.

Our apps

Each Baker Studios app may have different features and data practices. Nudge has a dedicated Nudge Privacy Policy, and Milo has a dedicated Milo Privacy Policy. Each describes the app’s current data handling, connected services, retention, deletion, and user choices.

We keep the information published here and in App Store Connect consistent with each app’s actual behavior.

Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate this website or respond to support requests, subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection obligations. We may also disclose information if reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, investigate abuse, or complete a business transaction such as a merger or acquisition.

Retention and deletion

We keep support emails only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or meet legal obligations. Server logs are retained only for a limited period needed for security and operations. You may ask us to delete personal information you sent us, subject to information we must retain for legal or legitimate business reasons.

Your choices and rights

You can choose not to email us. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, email apps@bakerstudios.net. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

Children’s privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Information may be processed in the United States or another country where our service providers operate, subject to applicable safeguards.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website, apps, or legal obligations change. We will post the revised policy here and update the date above. Material changes to an app’s data practices will be reflected before or when those changes take effect.

Contact us

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to apps@bakerstudios.net.
