Something to look forward to

Wait well
with Bimble.

Pick something you're looking forward to: Thanksgiving, winter break, a visit home. Pacer, a little red panda, starts walking a painted trail toward it, a step closer every day, whether or not you open the app. Behind him, the trail slowly turns into a village.

The trail is time

Nothing here can be broken.

No streaks, no score, no percentage. Pacer moves with the calendar, so a quiet day costs you nothing.

✦

Light the trail

Choose three small things you could manage even on a hard day. Each one you do lights today's spot on the trail: a candle, then a lantern. A rest day is a smooth stone, never a gap.

⌂

Grow a village

Every other day you show up, Pacer finds a new piece of the village. You choose what it is and where it goes, then walk inside to meet the neighbors and help with their errands.

❀

A place that changes

A hand-painted world that follows the real seasons and the time of day, with lanterns that come on in the evening and an outfit for the holidays.

Made for the wait

For the stretch between now and the day you're counting on.

Bimble was made for a first term away from home, but it works for any wait worth having: a trip, a visit, a holiday, a reunion. Add several stops along the way, write yourself a letter that stays sealed until you arrive, and keep a line for any day worth remembering.

Widgets put the trail and your three on the Home Screen, so you can see how far Pacer has come and check something off without opening the app. There is no account and no tracking. Your walk stays on your phone.
