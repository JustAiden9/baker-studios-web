A friendly coach

Your shoes have different jobs. Milo remembers.

Daily trainers, speed shoes, trail shoes, and race-day pairs wear differently and recover differently. Milo keeps your closet organized, makes your run history easier to understand, and helps spread work across your rotation.

Widgets, reminders, and plain-language insights keep the useful information close without turning every run into homework.
