Pip is on watch

Deadlines stay visible. Your day stays yours.

Nudge rechecks a connected calendar when you open or refresh the app, calls out moved due dates, and schedules reminders on your iPhone. The widget keeps the next deadline close without putting your private calendar link or assignment notes on the Home Screen.

An account is optional. Without one, your assignments, routine, plans, and history stay on your device. Beyond the account details needed to sign you in, Nudge saves the calendar link needed to restore your setup on another phone.
