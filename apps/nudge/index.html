Nudge: Assignment Reminders

Homework reminders before the last minute.

Nudge is a free iPhone and iPad app that brings every due date from your school’s platform into one list and reminds you before each deadline. Paste one private link from Canvas, Schoology, Blackboard, Brightspace, Moodle and more, and Pip, a small bird on your windowsill, nudges you early enough to actually do the work.

How Nudge works

Set it up once. Get nudged all term.

Nudge never asks for your school password. It reads the same private calendar link your school gives you for Apple Calendar or Google Calendar.

  1. Pick your school’s platformNudge shows you exactly where its private calendar link lives, button by button.
  2. Paste the link onceEvery dated assignment, quiz and exam shows up in one list, sorted by due date, across all your classes.
  3. Pip nudges you in timeA tap before each deadline, early enough to start. If a teacher moves a due date, Pip tells you what changed.

Why Nudge exists

It started with one missed assignment.

“I made Nudge after forgetting one assignment. It was on my Canvas list, but I needed a reminder before it was too late.”

A list only helps when you look at it. Nudge grew from that simple idea: tap students on the shoulder while there’s still time to do the work. Pip, the little bird in his nest, cheers when you finish something, naps when nothing’s due, and gets dizzy if you boop him too much.

Questions

Nudge FAQ

What is Nudge?

Nudge (full name Nudge: Assignment Reminders) is a free iPhone and iPad app that brings every due date from your school’s learning platform into one list and reminds you before each deadline. It’s made by Baker Studios, an independent app studio in Barrington, Illinois, and its mascot is Pip, a small bird who lives in a nest on your windowsill.

Is Nudge free?

Yes. Connecting your school, the due-date list, the live countdown and deadline reminders are free. Nudge Early Bird is an optional subscription that adds a morning brief, Plan it out for big assignments, earlier nudges, widgets and a Lock Screen countdown. The yearly plan starts with a 1-month free trial.

Which school platforms does Nudge work with?

Canvas, Schoology, Blackboard, Brightspace (D2L), Moodle, Sakai, ManageBac, Veracross and Blackbaud (myschoolapp), plus any other school system that gives you a private calendar link (iCal, webcal or .ics). You can also add assignments yourself by typing them like a text.

Does Nudge need my school password?

No. Nudge never signs in to your school. It reads the same private calendar link your school gives you for Apple Calendar or Google Calendar, from your phone.

Does Nudge work with Google Classroom?

Not at the moment. School Google accounts usually hide the private calendar address that Nudge would need. You can still add Classroom work to Nudge by typing it in.

Does Nudge know when I’ve submitted something?

No. School calendar feeds share due dates, not submission status. Mark work done in Nudge after you hand it in; Mark all done clears an old backlog in one tap. Nothing you do in Nudge changes anything in your school’s platform.

When will Pip remind me?

You choose. “Just a heads-up” nudges 1 day and 2 hours before a deadline; “The day before” nudges 1 day, 3 hours and 1 hour before; Early Bird adds a nudge 2 days early. Deadlines due together share one nudge, and nothing arrives overnight: late-night nudges move to the evening before.

Is Nudge on Android?

No. Nudge is available for iPhone and iPad (iOS and iPadOS 17 or later) on the App Store.

Is this the same as other apps called Nudge?

There are several unrelated apps named Nudge. This one is Nudge: Assignment Reminders by Baker Studios LLC: the homework and deadline app with Pip the bird. Its App Store ID is 6812403063.
