Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Baker Studios LLC, doing business as Baker Studios (“Baker Studios,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use Nudge.

The short version: Nudge works without an account. Assignments, notes, routines, plans, and completion history stay on your device. If you create an optional account, Nudge stores your account details, your private school calendar link, and your Nudge Early Bird subscription status so you can restore them on another phone. Apple handles all subscription payments; we never see your payment details. Nudge has no advertising or third-party analytics and does not sell personal information or track you across apps and websites.

Information stored on your device

Nudge stores information you add or import, including assignment titles, courses, due dates, notes, source links, completion status, deadline changes, reminder choices, planned focus sessions, routine and quiet-hour settings, notification preferences, and calendar-sync status. It also stores the school calendar feed URL you provide. That URL can contain a private access token, so Nudge protects its local data file using iOS file protection.

This information powers your deadline list, reminders, daily summaries, focus plans, deadline-change notices, and widgets. Nudge does not upload your assignments, notes, routines, focus plans, or completion history to a Nudge account.

School calendar imports

You may import an iCalendar file or provide an HTTPS calendar feed URL, such as a calendar feed from Canvas, Schoology, Blackboard, Brightspace, Moodle, Sakai, ManageBac, Veracross or Blackbaud. When you connect a feed, Nudge downloads it directly from the address you provide when you connect, refresh, or use supported background refresh. The calendar provider and its network providers may receive ordinary request information such as your IP address, request time, and device or network details under their own policies.

Nudge reads supported event details from the feed, including assignment title, course, due date, notes or description, source URL, event identifier, cancellation status, and deadline changes. Calendar feeds may not report whether work was submitted, so completion status in Nudge is controlled by you. Nudge does not modify your courses, assignments, or submissions in your school’s platform.

Optional Nudge account

You can use Nudge without an account. If you sign in with Apple, Google, or an email code, our backend provider, Supabase, processes and stores account information such as your user identifier, email address, name when provided by the sign-in service, authentication provider, authentication credentials or tokens needed to maintain a session, and related security logs. Apple or Google also processes your sign-in under its own terms when you choose that option. Resend delivers sign-in-code emails and may process your email address and delivery information for that purpose.

While you are signed in, Nudge stores the school calendar feed URL associated with your account in Supabase. Because that URL can contain a private token, it is treated as sensitive. Row-level access controls are designed so an authenticated account can access only its own profile. The saved link lets Nudge restore the connection on another phone; imported assignments themselves remain on your device.

Nudge Early Bird subscription

Nudge Early Bird is an optional auto-renewable subscription. Apple processes the purchase, free trial, renewal, cancellation, and any refund through your Apple Account under Apple’s terms. We do not receive your name, card number, billing address, or other payment details from Apple.

On your device, Nudge uses Apple’s StoreKit to check whether you have an active subscription or free trial, which plan it is, and when it renews or ends, and keeps that status locally so Early Bird features work offline.

While you are signed in to an optional account, Nudge also stores an Early Bird record in Supabase: your subscription status (none, trial, active, or lapsed), plan, renewal or end date, whether it is set to renew, Apple’s original transaction identifier for the subscription, and whether you are an early Nudge user who keeps Early Bird free. We use this record to restore your Early Bird access on another phone, honor free access for early users, answer support requests, and prevent abuse. It is not used for advertising or tracking. Row-level access controls are designed so an authenticated account can read only its own record, and the app cannot grant itself early-user access.

Notifications, background refresh, and widgets

If you grant notification permission, Nudge schedules deadline reminders, daily summaries or a morning brief, focus-session reminders, snoozed follow-ups, deadline-change notices, and a reminder before a free trial ends on your device. With Early Bird, Nudge can also show a live deadline countdown on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island using iOS Live Activities. Notification content may include an assignment title, course, due time, or a short step you entered and may be visible on your Lock Screen depending on your iOS settings.

Nudge may ask iOS for background time to refresh a connected calendar feed. iOS decides whether and when that refresh runs. Nudge’s widget uses a shared on-device app container for a limited assignment snapshot and completion count. It does not include assignment notes or your calendar feed URL.

How information is used

We use information only to provide and secure the features you request: authenticating an optional account, saving or restoring the connected feed URL and Early Bird status, providing Early Bird features, importing deadlines, scheduling local reminders, displaying widgets, responding to support, preventing abuse, and diagnosing service failures. We do not use Nudge information for targeted advertising, data brokerage, or cross-app tracking.

How information is shared

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only as needed to provide a feature you choose, comply with law, protect rights or safety, or operate and secure the service. Current providers and platforms include:

  • Apple, for Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases and subscriptions, notifications, widgets, Live Activities, background refresh, and other iOS services.
  • Google, when you choose Google Sign-In.
  • Supabase, for optional account authentication, the saved calendar feed URL, the Early Bird subscription record, and related security and service logs.
  • Resend, for delivery of optional email sign-in codes and links.
  • Your calendar provider, such as Canvas, Schoology, Blackboard or your school’s hosting provider, when Nudge requests the calendar feed URL you supplied.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy commitments. Nudge is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Instructure or your educational institution.

Retention and deletion

On-device data remains until you delete individual items, use “Erase everything,” or remove Nudge and its data from your device. “Erase everything” deletes Nudge assignments and finished history on that device and disconnects the calendar feed; it does not alter anything in your school’s platform and does not by itself delete an optional account.

If you created an account, you can permanently delete it in Nudge’s settings. Account deletion removes the account, its saved calendar feed URL, and its Early Bird record from our backend and signs you out. Deleting your account does not cancel an Early Bird subscription; Apple bills it until you cancel it in your Apple Account settings. Assignments and other local information on that phone remain so Nudge can continue working without an account; use “Erase everything” separately if you also want to remove them. Provider operational, security, and email-delivery logs may remain under provider settings and legal obligations. Limited records may also be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary to prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or protect the service.

Your choices and rights

Account creation, calendar connection, notifications, background refresh, and widgets are optional. You can disconnect the calendar feed, deny or change notifications in Nudge or iOS Settings, remove widgets, sign out, delete your account, and erase on-device Nudge data using the available controls.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal information. To make a privacy request, email apps@bakerstudios.net. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Children’s privacy

Nudge is a general-purpose organization tool that may be useful to students. We do not knowingly collect online personal information from a child under 13 without the consent required by law. If you are not old enough to consent to an optional account where you live, do not create one without permission from a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided personal information without appropriate consent should contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information, including iOS file protection for Nudge’s local data and access controls for account profiles. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device, email and sign-in accounts, and school calendar link. Information may be processed in the United States or another country where our providers operate, subject to applicable safeguards.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Nudge, its providers, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy at this URL and update the date above. Material changes will be reflected before or when the related app behavior takes effect.

Contact

Questions and privacy requests can be sent to apps@bakerstudios.net.
