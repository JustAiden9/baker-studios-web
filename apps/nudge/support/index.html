About Nudge

Contact Nudge support

Email apps@bakerstudios.net and we’ll aim to respond within two business days. Please include the Nudge version, your iPhone model and iOS version, what you expected, what happened, and any steps that reproduce the issue.

Please do not email passwords, sign-in codes, your private Canvas calendar link, complete assignment notes, or other sensitive information.

Common questions

How do I connect Canvas?

In Canvas, copy your Calendar Feed link, then open Nudge’s Canvas setup and paste it there. Use the private HTTPS calendar-feed link—not a course-page address. You can also import a supported .ics calendar file.

Why is an assignment missing?

Pull down on Nudge’s Home screen to refresh and make sure the Canvas connection still shows as connected. Canvas calendar feeds include dated events; undated work or items your institution omits from the feed will not appear automatically. You can add an assignment manually instead.

Why does finished work still appear?

Canvas calendar feeds provide due dates but do not tell Nudge what you submitted. Mark an assignment finished in Nudge after handing it in. This changes only Nudge and does not submit or alter work in Canvas.

Why didn’t I receive a reminder?

Check that Deadline nudges are enabled in Nudge, that iOS allows notifications, and that an iOS Focus is not silencing them. Reminders move outside your quiet hours, and iOS ultimately controls delivery timing. Open Nudge to refresh your deadlines and reminder schedule.

Can I use Nudge without an account?

Yes. Assignments, reminders, planning, and widgets work without an account. Signing in is optional and saves your Canvas calendar link so that connection can be restored on another phone.

How do I disconnect Canvas or erase my data?

Open Nudge Settings to disconnect the Canvas feed or choose “Erase everything” to remove assignments and finished history from that phone. Neither action changes anything in Canvas.

Privacy and account deletion

Read the Nudge Privacy Policy for details. If you created an account, choose “Delete account” in Nudge Settings to delete the account and saved Canvas link. Local assignments remain until you erase them separately. You can also email apps@bakerstudios.net with a privacy request.

Important reminder

Nudge is an organizational aid, not an official academic record or guaranteed alarm system. Confirm due dates and submission status in Canvas or directly with your school and instructors.
