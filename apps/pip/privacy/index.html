Overview

Pip was previously named Nudge. This policy applies to the app under either name.

This Privacy Policy explains how Baker Studios LLC, doing business as Baker Studios (“Baker Studios,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use Pip.

The short version: Pip works without an account. Assignments, notes, routines, plans, and completion history stay on your device. If you create an optional account, Pip stores your account details and private Canvas calendar link so you can restore that connection on another phone. Pip has no advertising or third-party analytics and does not sell personal information or track you across apps and websites.

Information stored on your device

Pip stores information you add or import, including assignment titles, courses, due dates, notes, source links, completion status, deadline changes, reminder choices, planned focus sessions, routine and quiet-hour settings, notification preferences, and calendar-sync status. It also stores the Canvas calendar feed URL you provide. That URL can contain a private access token, so Pip protects its local data file using iOS file protection.

This information powers your deadline list, reminders, daily summaries, focus plans, deadline-change notices, and widgets. Pip does not upload your assignments, notes, routines, focus plans, or completion history to a Pip account.

Canvas calendar imports

You may import an iCalendar file or provide an HTTPS calendar feed URL, such as a Canvas calendar feed. When you connect a feed, Pip downloads it directly from the address you provide when you connect, refresh, or use supported background refresh. The calendar provider and its network providers may receive ordinary request information such as your IP address, request time, and device or network details under their own policies.

Pip reads supported event details from the feed, including assignment title, course, due date, notes or description, source URL, event identifier, cancellation status, and deadline changes. Calendar feeds may not report whether work was submitted, so completion status in Pip is controlled by you. Pip does not modify your courses, assignments, or submissions in Canvas.

Optional Pip account

You can use Pip without an account. If you sign in with Apple, Google, or an email code, our backend provider, Supabase, processes and stores account information such as your user identifier, email address, name when provided by the sign-in service, authentication provider, authentication credentials or tokens needed to maintain a session, and related security logs. Apple or Google also processes your sign-in under its own terms when you choose that option. Resend delivers sign-in-code emails and may process your email address and delivery information for that purpose.

While you are signed in, Pip stores the Canvas calendar feed URL associated with your account in Supabase. Because that URL can contain a private token, it is treated as sensitive. Row-level access controls are designed so an authenticated account can access only its own profile. The saved link lets Pip restore the connection on another phone; imported assignments themselves remain on your device.

Notifications, background refresh, and widgets

If you grant notification permission, Pip schedules deadline reminders, daily summaries, focus-session reminders, snoozed follow-ups, and deadline-change notices on your device. Notification content may include an assignment title, course, due time, or a short step you entered and may be visible on your Lock Screen depending on your iOS settings.

Pip may ask iOS for background time to refresh a connected calendar feed. iOS decides whether and when that refresh runs. Pip’s widget uses a shared on-device app container for a limited assignment snapshot and completion count. It does not include assignment notes or your Canvas feed URL.

How information is used

We use information only to provide and secure the features you request: authenticating an optional account, saving or restoring the connected feed URL, importing deadlines, scheduling local reminders, displaying widgets, responding to support, preventing abuse, and diagnosing service failures. We do not use Pip information for targeted advertising, data brokerage, or cross-app tracking.

How information is shared

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only as needed to provide a feature you choose, comply with law, protect rights or safety, or operate and secure the service. Current providers and platforms include:

  • Apple, for Sign in with Apple, notifications, widgets, background refresh, and other iOS services.
  • Google, when you choose Google Sign-In.
  • Supabase, for optional account authentication, the saved Canvas feed URL, and related security and service logs.
  • Resend, for delivery of optional email sign-in codes and links.
  • Your calendar provider, such as Canvas or your school’s hosting provider, when Pip requests the calendar feed URL you supplied.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy commitments. Pip is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Instructure or your educational institution.

Retention and deletion

On-device data remains until you delete individual items, use “Erase everything,” or remove Pip and its data from your device. “Erase everything” deletes Pip assignments and finished history on that device and disconnects the Canvas feed; it does not alter anything in Canvas and does not by itself delete an optional account.

If you created an account, you can permanently delete it in Pip’s settings. Account deletion removes the account and its saved Canvas feed URL from our backend and signs you out. Assignments and other local information on that phone remain so Pip can continue working without an account; use “Erase everything” separately if you also want to remove them. Provider operational, security, and email-delivery logs may remain under provider settings and legal obligations. Limited records may also be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary to prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or protect the service.

Your choices and rights

Account creation, calendar connection, notifications, background refresh, and widgets are optional. You can disconnect the calendar feed, deny or change notifications in Pip or iOS Settings, remove widgets, sign out, delete your account, and erase on-device Pip data using the available controls.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal information. To make a privacy request, email apps@bakerstudios.net. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Children’s privacy

Pip is a general-purpose organization tool that may be useful to students. We do not knowingly collect online personal information from a child under 13 without the consent required by law. If you are not old enough to consent to an optional account where you live, do not create one without permission from a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided personal information without appropriate consent should contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information, including iOS file protection for Pip’s local data and access controls for account profiles. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device, email and sign-in accounts, and Canvas calendar link. Information may be processed in the United States or another country where our providers operate, subject to applicable safeguards.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Pip, its providers, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy at this URL and update the date above. Material changes will be reflected before or when the related app behavior takes effect.

Contact

Questions and privacy requests can be sent to apps@bakerstudios.net.
