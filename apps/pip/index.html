Meet Pip

Read the crest before the clock.

Pip’s two-leaf crest is a status light. You can tell how your day is going before you read a single number.

Relaxed

Nothing due today. All clear.

Straight up

Something’s due today, or within three hours.

Drooping

Something slipped. It still counts if you send it.

Nightcap

All clear, and it’s late. Pip’s off duty.

What Pip does

One job: what’s due next, and how long you have.

No planner to maintain and no dashboard to check. Pip does the typing and speaks up when it matters.

Canvas does the typing

Paste your Canvas calendar feed once. Pip brings in every dated assignment and checks for changes when you open the app.

A countdown, front and center

Home opens on the next deadline you can still make: 3h 12m until Problem set 04 is due.

Nudges that fit your day

Choose how early Pip speaks up. Nudges stay out of your quiet hours, merge when they’re close, and you get one summary on days something’s due.

“Remind me after work”

Press and hold a nudge and type when you’ll actually get to it. Pip reads it back as a real time, then quotes you when it’s time.

Type it like a text

Quick add reads ECON hw tonight at 9 and saves the class, the title and the time. No form.

Keep it in sight

Widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen show the countdown and Pip, never your Canvas link or your notes.

Small taps, not a dashboard

Pip speaks up when something changes.

A line from Pip or a single notification, then he gets out of your way.

A deadline moved

When Canvas changes a due date, Pip shows the old date and the new one, and every nudge follows the new time.

Big work, started early

Essays, projects and exams get one tap on the shoulder two days before: 30 minutes now makes Friday a lot easier.

Done is done

Mark something finished and Pip hops. Canvas feeds don’t know what you’ve turned in, so a whole backlog clears with one tap, with Undo.

Connect Canvas

Three steps, about a minute.

Canvas gives every student a private calendar feed. Pip reads it straight from your phone.

  1. Open your calendar

    In Canvas, go to Calendar, then Calendar Feed.

  2. Copy the link

    Copy the whole HTTPS link it shows. It’s the feed for every course you’re in.

  3. Paste it into Pip

    Pip brings in your deadlines and asks once whether past ones are already turned in.

Keep that link to yourself

The feed link works like a key to your calendar. Don’t post it or send it to anyone, including support.

No Canvas? No problem.

Add work yourself in one line with quick add, or import an .ics calendar file.

Why Pip exists

Privacy

Your homework stays on your phone.

Pip works without an account. Signing in is optional and only saves what it needs to bring Canvas back on a new phone.

Without an account

  • Assignments, notes, your routine, plans and history stay on your device.
  • Pip downloads your Canvas feed directly from your phone.
  • Widgets never show your Canvas link or notes.

With an optional account

  • Sign in with Apple, Google or an emailed code.
  • Your account keeps your sign-in details and your Canvas link. Assignments stay on your phone.
  • No ads, no third-party analytics, no tracking. Delete the account any time in Settings.
