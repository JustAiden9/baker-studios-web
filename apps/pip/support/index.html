Contact Pip support

Email apps@bakerstudios.net and we’ll aim to respond within two business days. Please include the Pip version, your iPhone model and iOS version, what you expected, what happened, and any steps that reproduce the issue.

Please do not email passwords, sign-in codes, your private Canvas calendar link, complete assignment notes, or other sensitive information.

Common questions

How do I connect Canvas?

In Canvas, open Calendar, then Calendar Feed, and copy the link. Paste it into Pip’s Canvas setup. Use the private HTTPS calendar-feed link, not a course-page address. Keep that link private: anyone who has it can see your calendar. You can also import a supported .ics calendar file, or add assignments yourself.

Why didn’t I get a nudge?

Check that Pip is allowed to send notifications in iOS Settings, that Deadline nudges are on in Pip, and that an iOS Focus isn’t silencing them. Nudges that would land in your quiet hours move to before bedtime, and iOS decides exactly when notifications are delivered. Open Pip to refresh your deadlines and nudge schedule.

Why is an assignment missing?

Make sure it appears in your Canvas calendar feed. Pull down on Pip’s home screen to refresh, and check in Settings that the Canvas connection is still there. Canvas feeds only include dated items, and your school may leave some work out. You can add an assignment yourself with quick add.

I already turned it in. Why is it still there?

Canvas calendar feeds carry due dates, not submissions, so Pip can’t tell what you’ve handed in. Mark it done in Pip, or use Mark all done on past-due work. This changes only Pip; it doesn’t submit or change anything in Canvas.

A deadline is wrong or moved.

Open Pip to refresh. When Canvas changes a due date, Pip shows what moved and its nudges follow the new time. If a date still looks wrong, check the assignment in Canvas; Pip shows what the feed says.

Can I use Pip without an account?

Yes. Assignments, nudges, planning and widgets all work without an account. Signing in is optional and saves your Canvas calendar link so the connection can come back on another phone.

How do I delete my account or erase my data?

To delete an account, go to Settings, then Account, then Delete account. That removes the account and the saved Canvas link. Assignments on your phone are separate: use Erase everything in Settings to remove them and disconnect Canvas. Neither action changes anything in Canvas.

Is this the app that used to be called Nudge?

Yes. Pip is Nudge with a new name, a new look and a new character. It’s the same app on the App Store.

Privacy and account deletion

Read the Pip Privacy Policy for details. If you created an account, choose Delete account in Pip’s Settings to delete the account and saved Canvas link. Local assignments remain until you erase them separately. You can also email apps@bakerstudios.net with a privacy request.

Important reminder

Pip is an organizational aid, not an official academic record or guaranteed alarm system. Confirm due dates and submission status in Canvas or directly with your school and instructors.
