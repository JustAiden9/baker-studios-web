Get in touch

If you have a question, found an issue, or need help with a Baker Studios app, email us. We read every message and aim to respond within two business days.

What to include

To help us understand an app issue, please include:

  • The app name and version
  • Your device model and operating-system version
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead
  • Steps we can follow to reproduce the issue

Please do not include passwords, payment-card details, health records, or other sensitive information in your email.

Privacy and data requests

For questions about privacy or to make a data request, email apps@bakerstudios.net. Read our Privacy Policy for more information.
